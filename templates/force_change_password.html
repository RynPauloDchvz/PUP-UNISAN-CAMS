Biometric Guidelines

Important instructions for your facial enrollment

Real Live Face Only Strictly live person only. 2x2 photos, printed pictures, screens, animals, or objects are strictly prohibited and will be rejected.
No Accessories or Coverings Remove eyeglasses, sunglasses, face masks, face coverings, or hats for accurate feature detection.
Even, Clear Lighting Ensure good front lighting. Avoid dim lighting, dark rooms, or strong backlighting/glare.
Centered & Single Person Position your face inside the target frame. Only 1 person must be present in the camera view.
